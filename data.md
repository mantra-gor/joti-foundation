2,456 Total People Reacher

KEY ACTIVITIES: 
Stakeholder Consultation & Social Mobilisation: Village Sarpanches, ASHA workers, Anganwadi workers, school teachers, and community leaders engaged across 10 villages.

Early Warning Dissemination Groups (EWIDGs): 10 EWIDGs formed one per village  to receive IMD alerts and disseminate cold wave warnings to every household.

1,958 Individuals in Awareness Sessions: 19 sessions covering cold wave risks, insulation techniques, safe heating, layered clothing, and protection of elderly and children.

Insulation Support Kits for 10 Vulnerable HH: Bubble insulation sheets, canvas tarpaulins, green shade nets, and mink blankets for the most economically vulnerable.
